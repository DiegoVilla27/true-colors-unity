Official Privacy Policy & Legal Information

Developer: BBGames GDPR & CCPA Compliant COPPA Friendly Last Updated: Sep 29, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy describes how BBGames ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information in connection with the mobile application TrueColors ("the Game").

We believe in player privacy and data minimization. We do not require account registration with personal identifiers such as your real name, address, or email to enjoy the game.

2. Information Collected & Purpose

A. Anonymous Authentication

When you play TrueColors, an anonymous, unique player ID is generated by Unity Authentication. This identifier allows you to preserve your game profile and settings across sessions without collecting private personal credentials.

B. Cloud Save & Game Progress

We store in-game currency balances, high scores, unlocked starship skins, and audio preferences through Unity Cloud Save. This ensures you never lose progress if you switch devices or reinstall the Game.

C. Global Leaderboards

High scores and chosen nicknames are submitted to Unity Leaderboards. Nicknames are publicly visible on rankings tables; players are advised not to include personal names or sensitive details in their nickname.

D. Advertisements & Monetization

TrueColors serves rewarded and interstitial ads via Unity LevelPlay (ironSource / Unity Technologies). These services may collect non-sensitive technical data such as device advertising identifiers (GAID / IDFA), IP address (for general geographic region), and ad interaction telemetry to serve contextually relevant ads and prevent fraud.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The Game integrates trusted third-party SDKs that operate under their own privacy standards:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services & Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • ironSource / LevelPlay: ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc.: Apple Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

TrueColors is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). If we discover that such data has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we take immediate action to remove it.

5. Player Rights & Data Control

Under regulations like GDPR (EEA) and CCPA / CPRA (California), you have the right to:

  • Access or export the data stored about your gameplay session.
  • Request permanent deletion of your player profile and cloud save records.
  • Opt out of targeted advertisements through your device settings:
    • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
    • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID.

To request data deletion:

Email us with your anonymous Player ID (visible in Game Settings) at:

6. Security & Policy Updates

We use secure, encrypted transmission protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for all network communication. We may update this policy periodically; changes will always be reflected on this page with an updated timestamp.
