# Privacy Policy for TrueColors

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026  
**Effective date:** September 29, 2026  

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game **TrueColors** ("the Game", "the Application"), developed and published by **BBGames** ("we", "us", or "our"). 

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected through your use of TrueColors. This document outlines the types of information we collect, how it is processed and protected, and your rights regarding your data in accordance with applicable laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**), the California Consumer Privacy Act (**CCPA** / **CPRA**), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (**COPPA**).

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

TrueColors is designed to minimize personal data collection. We do not require you to provide personally identifiable information such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number to play the game.

### A. Anonymous Authentication & Player Identity
* **Data collected:** A randomized, anonymous player identifier generated by Unity Authentication.
* **Purpose:** To maintain your unique game profile, cloud progression, and settings across game sessions without requiring personal accounts or passwords.
* **Provider:** Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies).

### B. Game Progress & Save Data (Cloud Save)
* **Data collected:** In-game currency balances (stars/coins), high scores, unlocked cosmetic skins (starships), audio preferences, and gameplay milestones.
* **Purpose:** To persist your game progress and prevent data loss if you switch devices or reinstall the Game.
* **Provider:** Unity Gaming Services (Cloud Save).

### C. Leaderboards & Competitive Records
* **Data collected:** Player display nickname (anonymous or chosen by the player), high scores, leaderboard rank, and record submission timestamps.
* **Purpose:** To display global and regional rankings. Display names must not contain real personal information.
* **Provider:** Unity Gaming Services (Leaderboards).

### D. Advertising and Monetization (Rewarded & Interstitial Ads)
* **Data collected:** Non-precise device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID on Android, Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA on iOS if permitted), IP address (truncated/coarse location), device type, operating system version, and ad interaction metrics (e.g., ad views, clicks, and completion rates).
* **Purpose:** To deliver rewarded video ads (e.g., reviving the player or earning in-game currency) and interstitial ads, as well as to prevent ad fraud and frequency-cap ads.
* **Provider:** Unity LevelPlay (ironSource / Unity Technologies) and integrated ad network partners.

### E. Diagnostic & Performance Data
* **Data collected:** Crash reports, frame rate metrics, and basic device hardware specifications.
* **Purpose:** To identify technical glitches, optimize performance across different devices, and improve game stability.

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

TrueColors is a general-audience arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). 

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately. Upon notification, we will take swift measures to delete any such data from our records and servers.

## 4. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your country or state of residence, you may have specific privacy rights:

### A. European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Users (GDPR)
* **Right to Access:** You may request confirmation and copies of your stored game data.
* **Right to Rectification:** You can update your display name or game preferences at any time in the settings menu.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You can request the permanent deletion of your player profile, cloud save data, and leaderboard entries.
* **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent (such as personalized ads), you can withdraw consent at any time via your device settings.

### B. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)
* You have the right to know what personal data is collected and how it is used.
* We do **not** sell your personal information for monetary consideration.
* You have the right to request deletion of your data and to be free from discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

### C. How to Control Advertising Tracking on Your Device
You can opt out of personalized advertisements directly through your mobile device settings:
* **iOS:** Go to *Settings* > *Privacy & Security* > *Tracking*, and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
* **Android:** Go to *Settings* > *Privacy* (or *Google*) > *Ads*, and select "Delete advertising ID" or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
